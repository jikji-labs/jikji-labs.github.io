Design stage — before implementation

Connected state, from execution to research evidence

State, memory, tool results, experiments and claims are designed as entities with graph identities and provenance from the beginning.

Canonical SQL, authorized knowledge views

Typed SQL rows and quads are canonical. State, events, graph assertions and the outbox share a transaction; the knowledge engine reads authorized projections. A lagging projection must not decide current permissions.

The lifecycle of a scientific claim

Hypotheses connect to experiments and protocols, datasets and artifacts, verification and independent review, and supported, refuted or contested outcomes. Memory carries evidence, validity periods and verification state. Model suggestions begin unverified.

Schema validity and scientific validity

SHACL, provenance and bounded queries require technical validation. A valid schema does not establish scientific truth; experimental oracles and review remain necessary. Knowledge-engine APIs and resource costs still require spikes.
