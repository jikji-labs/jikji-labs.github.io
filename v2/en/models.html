Design stage — before implementation

Route models within quality, policy and budget

Models are selected for roles and task requirements after checking data classification, authorization, tool capabilities and available quota.

Role profiles and independent review

The plan compares GLM, Qwen, GPT and DeepSeek families on real tasks. Reviewers should preferably use an independent model family. Substitution requires capability and availability checks; lower cost alone cannot justify an unvalidated quality downgrade.

Reservations and fairness

Quota snapshots retain time, units and source. Root and child reservations must remain within budget. Weighted fair queues with aging are proposed; stale metering defers new expensive calls. Cancellation does not erase charges, so final usage must settle reservations.

Measurement boundaries

Existing bridge completion mode must be distinguished from a native v2 loop. Native streaming and tool-event contracts remain unresolved. Paired benchmarks must include latency, quality, tokens, memory and failed attempts; unavailable measurements remain missing.

All figures are provisional targets, not measurements. Korean is authoritative. Source: design documents 01–10, R1–R20; editorial snapshot 2026-10-18.
