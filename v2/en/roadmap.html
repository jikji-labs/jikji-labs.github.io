Design stage — before implementation

From design to validation, gate by gate

Current status is G0 design review. The stages below are plans, not claims of completed implementation, performance or deployment.

G0 → G2 · Design and local foundation

G0: source and license inventory, R1–R20, ADRs, threat model and design review. G1: model-event, knowledge-engine, database and OS-sandbox spikes with baselines. G2: local loop, tools, receipts, graph and both database contract suites. Design review and implementation authorization remain pending.

G3 → G6 · Distributed validation and adoption

G3: server, workers, APIs, delegation, dashboard and fault, cancellation and authorization tests. G4: real-model paired benchmarks and independent quality checks. G5: opt-in pilots and recovery drills. G6: per-consumer promotion based on evidence and observation. Each gate requires validation and a separate adoption decision.
